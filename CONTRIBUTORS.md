# Contributors

`mvp` is made possible by the people who contribute code, tests, documentation, design, issue reports, and review.

## Project lead

- [Quinntyne Brown](https://github.com/QuinntyneBrown) — creator and lead maintainer

## Community contributors

See GitHub's [contributors graph](https://github.com/QuinntyneBrown/mvp/graphs/contributors) for the history of merged contributions.

Contributors with a merged change are welcome to add themselves here in a focused pull request:

```markdown
- [Your Name](https://github.com/your-handle) — code, documentation, design, testing, or community
```

Listing is optional and does not determine project roles or repository permissions. See [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) for role definitions and the path to maintainership.
