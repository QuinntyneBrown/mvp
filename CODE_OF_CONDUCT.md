# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The `mvp` community is committed to providing a welcoming, safe, and inclusive experience for everyone, regardless of identity, background, appearance, ability, experience, education, nationality, ethnicity, religion, caste, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, or socioeconomic status.

We commit to acting in ways that support a healthy, professional, and collaborative open-source community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that strengthens the community include:

- Showing empathy, patience, and respect.
- Welcoming different viewpoints and levels of experience.
- Giving specific, constructive feedback and receiving it in good faith.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes, repairing harm where possible, and learning from the experience.
- Focusing discussion on the work and the well-being of the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or sustained disruption.
- Discriminatory, insulting, sexualized, or derogatory language or imagery.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Personal or political attacks unrelated to the project.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including repositories, issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, community events, and private communications related to project work. It also applies when someone officially represents the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately to the [lead maintainer](https://github.com/QuinntyneBrown) using a private contact method listed on that profile. Do not open a public issue about a conduct incident.

Maintainers will review reports promptly, fairly, and with respect for the reporter's privacy and safety. Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this policy. They may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Anyone responsible for enforcing this policy must disclose a conflict of interest and allow another maintainer or a mutually agreed independent reviewer to handle the report.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will consider the context, severity, persistence, and impact of a violation. Responses may include a private correction, a formal warning, a temporary restriction, or a permanent ban. When appropriate, the maintainers will explain the decision to the affected parties without disclosing confidential information.
